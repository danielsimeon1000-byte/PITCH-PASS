Pitch Pass privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Microphone

Pitch Pass uses your microphone only while you are in a lesson, drill or range check, to measure the pitch of the note you sing. The sound is analysed on your device in real time and then discarded. Pitch Pass does not record, store or upload any audio.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (lessons completed, accuracy, streak, voice range and settings) is saved on your device so the app can remember where you are. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or using Reset all progress in Settings, removes it.

Purchases

If you buy Pitch Pass Pro, the purchase is handled by the App Store or Google Play. We receive confirmation that you have an active subscription, not your payment details.

Analytics and advertising

Pitch Pass has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Children

Pitch Pass does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
